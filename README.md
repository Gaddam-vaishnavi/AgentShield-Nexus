# AgentShield Nexus

## Collective Risk Intelligence for Agentic Commerce

> Detect coordinated agent behavior before it becomes an inventory loss.

AgentShield Nexus is a defense-first risk intelligence system for agentic commerce.

Instead of evaluating transactions individually, AgentShield analyzes behavior across multiple AI shopping agents. It combines purchase velocity, synchronization, inventory pressure, and behavioral coordination to identify collective risk and recommend an appropriate protection action.

## The Problem

As AI agents become capable of discovering products, making purchasing decisions, and completing transactions autonomously, a new commerce risk emerges.

A single agent may behave normally.

But hundreds of agents acting on the same product within milliseconds can create coordinated demand and rapidly exhaust scarce inventory.

**AgentShield looks at the group, not just the transaction.**
